🎱 Pool Cue Guide

Upload a photo of the table, check the detected balls, approve, get 3 shot options.

Photo of your table

From above if you can, whole felt in view, even light. JPG or PNG, up to 12 MB.

Best results: cover the six pockets with bright green (neon) discs. The app uses their centres as the table outline.

Ball labels (B1, B2…) are drawn on the picture. Magenta rings mark balls on your list.

Check the balls

Type a ball label (like B3) and press Add, or tap one under All detected balls. Then tick Improve (re-detect it) or Remove (it isn't a ball).
